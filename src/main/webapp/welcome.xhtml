What makes it different

A racing format built around efficiency

TOP-Racing is open to low-weight racing vehicles and encourages different designs, technologies and driving styles. The public standings show competition progress while promoters keep event safety, legality and organization under control.

Open technology

Participants can bring varied lightweight vehicles instead of racing under a single standardized design.

Efficiency prizes

Results value performance in relation to market value, so smart engineering can compete with larger budgets.

Public progress

Visitors can follow championship standings, event priorities and registration history from the public area of the site.
